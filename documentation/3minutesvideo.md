### SEGMENT 3: HOW IT WORKS — THE LEARNING LOOP
**[0:50 – 1:40] | 50 seconds**

> **VISUAL:** App demo — smooth screen recording showing the full learning loop.

**[VOICEOVER — descriptive, showing each step as it's mentioned]**

> *(Step 5 — Learn)*
> **VISUAL:** MIL lesson card — key takeaway, quiz question.
> "And then — a targeted Media Literacy lesson, connected directly to **this exact scenario**. Not a textbook. A lesson born from a mistake they just made."

> **VISUAL:** Skill Radar chart — five dimensions filling up.
> "Every session builds their **5-Dimension MIL Skill Radar** — tracking Source Verification, Evidence Evaluation, AI Literacy, Bias Detection, and Digital Safety."

## Visual Direction Notes

### App Demo Shots Needed
1. **Splash screen & onboarding** — 3-slide onboarding flow (3 seconds)
2. **Feed screen** — scroll through 2-3 post cards (5 seconds)
3. **TrustLens panel** — open and expand each section (10 seconds)
4. **Decision screen** — tap "VERIFY" or "SHARE" (3 seconds)
5. **Consequence card** — watch numbers animate (5 seconds)
6. **MIL lesson screen** — show key takeaway text (5 seconds)
7. **Skill Radar** — radar filling in after session (3 seconds)
8. **Teacher dashboard** — class skill analytics view (3 seconds)

### Text Overlay Style
- Font: Clean sans-serif (match FeedWise brand — Outfit font)
- Color: White text on dark background OR dark text on light cards
- Animate: Fade-in / slide-up
- Keep overlays to 4–6 words max per screen

### Music Suggestion
- Genre: Subtle electronic ambient / light cinematic
- Volume: 20–30% under voiceover
- Mood: Thoughtful, forward-moving, not dramatic
- Recommended: YouTube Audio Library — "Inspire" category

## Voiceover Recording Tips

- **Pace:** Speak at 130–140 words per minute — slightly slower than natural conversation
- **Tone:** Conversational but authoritative — like a TEDx talk
- **Emphasis:** Bold words in the script = verbal emphasis
- **Pauses:** Use 0.5-second pauses between sections
- **Recording:** Quiet room, quality microphone, pop filter — no echo
- **Multiple takes:** Record each segment separately for easier editing
- **Check:** Run final edit through caption auto-generator and correct errors
