# FeedWise — Introduction

> **"Don't Just Scroll. Think."**
> A Practical Media & Information Literacy Platform for the AI Era

---

## What is FeedWise?

FeedWise is an interactive, AI-assisted Media and Information Literacy (MIL) platform designed to transform how young people engage with digital information. Submitted as an entry to the **UNESCO Global MIL Week 2026 Youth Challenge**, FeedWise is not just an application — it is a complete learning ecosystem that simulates the real information environment young people face every day.

In an era where artificial intelligence can generate convincing misinformation in seconds, where social media amplifies falsehoods faster than truth, and where deepfakes blur the boundary between reality and fabrication, the ability to critically evaluate information has become a fundamental life skill. Yet most media literacy education remains theoretical — it tells young people *what to think*, rather than teaching them *how to think*.

**FeedWise changes that.** It places students inside a simulated social media feed — a safe, consequence-modelled environment — where they must investigate posts, evaluate evidence, make decisions, observe real-world consequences, and learn from each experience.

---

## The Core Idea

FeedWise is built around one powerful learning loop:

```
Feed → Investigate → Decide → Consequence → Learn → Improve → Repeat
```

Every interaction on the platform is a learning opportunity. A student sees a social media post. They have tools to investigate — who wrote it, when, what evidence exists, what the language signals. They then make a decision: Share, Verify, Report, or Ignore. The platform immediately shows them the consequence of that decision — reach numbers, credibility impact, spread of misinformation. Then it delivers a targeted MIL lesson connected to that specific scenario.

This **simulation-first, evidence-based, consequence-aware** approach makes FeedWise fundamentally different from any existing media literacy tool.

## Who Is FeedWise For?

FeedWise serves three interconnected user communities:

| User Type | Role on FeedWise |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Students / Youth (Age 14–25)** | Primary learners — they experience the feed, investigate, decide, and track skill growth |
| **Teachers / Educators** | Classroom integrators — they monitor student performance, assign challenges, identify skill gaps, and guide learning |
| **Administrators / MIL Professionals** | Platform stewards — they create and review scenarios, manage content quality, oversee the AI engine, and maintain the integrity of the learning environment |

---

## What Makes FeedWise Unique?

1. **Simulated Social Feed** — Not a textbook, not a quiz. A realistic information environment with engagement numbers, trending badges, and believable posts.

2. **TrustLens Investigation Panel** — A structured tool that walks users through source credibility, author identity, date verification, evidence quality, and language signals.

3. **Consequence Engine** — Every decision produces measurable simulated impact: reach numbers, shares, credibility scores. Students feel the weight of their choices.

4. **AI-Assisted, Human-Reviewed** — The AI engine analyzes text signals and assists content creation, but never issues final verdicts. Human MIL professionals review every scenario. Teaching users to trust AI verdicts would undermine FeedWise's own mission.

5. **5-Dimension Skill Radar** — Progress tracked across Source Verification, Evidence Evaluation, AI Literacy, Bias Detection, and Digital Safety — visualized as a living radar chart.

6. **Newsroom Mode** — Students experience the editorial perspective, deciding what to publish as an editor — building empathy for the challenges of responsible journalism.

7. **Real Teacher Integration** — Not a standalone app. A classroom tool. Teachers see individual student performance, skill gaps, and can assign targeted challenges.

8. **Community Submissions** — Advanced users can submit real-world claims for the community to investigate, guided by MIL professionals.

## Platform Architecture at a Glance

FeedWise is built on three integrated pillars:

| Pillar | Technology | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **User Application** | Flutter (Android + Web) | Student learning interface |
| **Admin & Teacher Studio** | Flutter Web | Educator and admin portal |
| **Backend API** | FastAPI (Python) + Supabase (PostgreSQL) | Data, intelligence, and security |

The backend integrates an AI/NLP engine that assists scenario analysis and lesson generation, while a robust Row Level Security model ensures every user sees only what they are authorized to see.

> **"In the age of AI, the most important skill is knowing how to think — not what to think."**
> — FeedWise Design Principle
